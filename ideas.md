TSA Theme
Video Game Design

Theme: Create a deep space-themed RPG where the primary method of progression is puzzle-solving rather than combat.

Challenge: Your game must include core RPG elements (such as character stats, a leveling system, or branching dialogue), but you may not include traditional combat. Instead, players must use their character's skills and stats to solve environmental, logical, or social puzzles to survive the dangers of deep space.

Ideas
Our puzzle solving is something to do with making the ship function. Maybe you need to solve puzzles manipulating parts of the ship to allow things like coolant engine fuel to flow, managing life support, etc…

Core RPG elements could be ship stats for our spaceship, like crew onboard, resources, ship design, etc… Branching dialogue would be something like crew members asking you about things that need to be done for the ship. The dangers in space are the problems with OH NO WE'RE IN SPACE WE NEED LIFE SUPPORT WHAT THE HECK DUDE.
